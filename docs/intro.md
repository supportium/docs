# Welcome to Supportium AI Documentation

Transform your customer support with AI-powered assistance that understands your business and helps your customers 24/7.

## What You'll Learn

This documentation will guide you through setting up, customizing, and optimizing your AI customer support agent. Whether you're a business owner looking to improve customer service or a developer integrating our APIs, you'll find step-by-step instructions and best practices here.

## Quick Start Guide

**Get your AI support agent running in under 30 minutes:**

1. **[Create Your AI Support Agent](/start-here/create-your-ai-support-agent)** *(2 minutes hands-on time)*
   - Set up your account and configure basic settings
   - Connect your website or knowledge base

2. **[Add Your AI Agent to Your Website](/start-here/add-your-ai-agent-to-your-website)** *(5 minutes)*
   - Install the chat widget on your website
   - Platform-specific guides for WordPress, Shopify, and more

3. **[Train Your AI](/features/sitesync-adding-and-syncing-your-website-content)** *(Automatic after setup)*
   - Your AI automatically learns from your website content
   - Add custom answers and improve responses over time

4. **[Go Live](/start-here/how-long-does-it-take-to-set-up)** *(30 minutes total)*
   - Test your AI agent before customers see it
   - Monitor and optimize performance
