# How Long Does It Take to Set Up?

Get your AI customer support agent running faster than you might expect. Here's a realistic timeline for the entire setup process.

## Quick Answer

**Total Time: 30-60 minutes**
- **Your hands-on time**: 2-5 minutes
- **Automatic processing**: 25-55 minutes (you can work on other things)

## Detailed Timeline

### Phase 1: Account & Configuration *(2-5 minutes)*

**What you do:**
- Create your Supportium AI account
- Configure your AI agent's basic settings
- Add your website URL and preferences
- Install the chat widget code on your site

**Time breakdown:**
- Account creation: 1 minute
- Agent configuration: 2-3 minutes  
- Website code installation: 1-2 minutes

:::tip **Pro Tip**
Have your website admin login ready to speed up the installation step.
:::

### Phase 2: Automatic Processing *(25-55 minutes)*

**What Supportium AI does automatically:**
- Discovers and crawls all your web pages
- Extracts and processes your content
- Builds your AI agent's knowledge base
- Runs quality and safety checks

**Processing time by website size:**

| Website Size | Pages | Processing Time | 
|-------------|--------|----------------|
| **Small** | Under 100 pages | 10-20 minutes |
| **Medium** | 100-500 pages | 20-45 minutes |
| **Large** | 500+ pages | 45-90 minutes |
| **Enterprise** | 1000+ pages | 1-2 hours |

### Phase 3: Testing & Launch *(5-10 minutes)*

**What you do:**
- Receive email notification when processing is complete
- Test your AI agent with sample questions
- Verify responses are accurate and helpful
- Go live for customers

---

## What Happens During Processing?

While you wait, Supportium AI is working hard behind the scenes:

### 1. **Website Discovery** *(40% of time)*
- Finds all publicly accessible pages on your site
- Follows internal links to discover content
- Respects robots.txt and crawl rules
- Maps your site's structure and navigation

### 2. **Content Extraction** *(30% of time)*
- Extracts text content from each page
- Identifies headings, paragraphs, and lists
- Filters out navigation and boilerplate content
- Preserves important formatting and structure

### 3. **Knowledge Processing** *(20% of time)*
- Organizes content into searchable segments
- Creates semantic understanding of your content
- Builds connections between related topics
- Optimizes for question-answering accuracy

### 4. **Quality Assurance** *(10% of time)*
- Tests AI responses for accuracy
- Runs safety and content policy checks
- Validates all system integrations
- Performs final optimization

:::info **What Affects Processing Time?**
- **Website size**: More pages = longer processing
- **Content complexity**: Rich content takes longer to process
- **Site speed**: Faster sites are crawled more quickly
- **Server load**: Peak times may add 10-20% to processing time
:::

## Potential Delays & Solutions

### Common Issues That Add Time

**Large Website (500+ pages)**
- *Expected*: 45-90 minutes processing
- *Solution*: Use content filters to focus on important pages only

**Slow Website Response**
- *Symptom*: Longer than expected processing times
- *Solution*: Optimize your website's loading speed

**Protected/Private Content**
- *Symptom*: AI can't find important information
- *Solution*: Ensure key pages are publicly accessible

**Complex Site Structure**
- *Symptom*: Processing takes longer than expected
- *Solution*: Create/update your sitemap.xml file

### Getting Help

**If processing takes longer than expected:**
1. Check your email for status updates
2. Verify your website is accessible and loading properly
3. Contact support if processing exceeds 2 hours without notification

:::warning **When to Contact Support**
Reach out immediately if:
- Processing stops with an error message
- You don't receive a completion email within the expected timeframe
- Your website has special requirements (login-protected content, etc.)
:::
