# Create Your AI Support Agent

Set up your AI customer support agent in just 2 minutes of hands-on time. This step-by-step guide will walk you through creating your account, configuring your agent, and connecting your business content.

## What You'll Accomplish

By the end of this guide, you'll have:
- ✅ A fully configured AI support agent  
- ✅ Your website content automatically synced
- ✅ A working chat widget ready to deploy
- ✅ Basic customization settings configured

**⏱️ Time Required**: 2 minutes hands-on setup + 30 minutes automatic processing

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account

### Sign Up Process

1. **Visit the Supportium AI Platform**
   - Go to [app.supportium.ai/signup](https://app.supportium.ai/signup)
   - Click "Get Started Free" to begin

2. **Enter Your Information**
   - Provide your email address
   - Choose a secure password
   - Enter your business name
   - Select your company size

3. **Verify Your Email**
   - Check your inbox for a verification email
   - Click the verification link to activate your account

:::tip **Pro Tip**
Use your business email address to ensure you receive important updates and notifications about your AI agent.
:::

---

## Step 2: Configure Your AI Agent

### Basic Settings

1. **Name Your AI Agent**
   - Choose a name that reflects your brand (e.g., "Alex from AcmeCorp Support")
   - This name will appear to customers in chat conversations

2. **Set Your Agent's Personality**
   - Choose the tone: Professional, Friendly, or Casual
   - Add any specific instructions for how your agent should behave
   - Example: "Always ask if the customer needs additional help before ending conversations"

3. **Configure Default Responses**
   - Welcome message: What customers see when they start a chat
   - Offline message: What to show when human agents aren't available  
   - Error message: Fallback response when the AI encounters issues

### Example Configuration
```
Agent Name: "Sarah from TechSupport"
Tone: "Friendly and Professional"
Welcome Message: "Hi! I'm Sarah, your AI assistant. I'm here to help with any questions about our products and services. How can I assist you today?"
```

---

## Step 3: Connect Your Content Sources

### Website Content Sync

1. **Add Your Website URL**
   - Enter your primary website URL (e.g., https://yourwebsite.com)
   - Supportium AI will automatically discover and index your pages

2. **Select Content Types** *(Optional)*
   - Help documentation and FAQs
   - Product pages and descriptions
   - About and company information pages
   - Blog articles (if relevant for support)

3. **Set Sync Preferences**
   - **Automatic Sync**: Keep your AI updated as you change your website
   - **Sync Frequency**: Daily, Weekly, or Manual
   - **Content Filters**: Exclude specific pages or sections

### Additional Content Sources *(Optional)*

You can also connect:
- **Knowledge Base**: Import from tools like Zendesk, Freshdesk, or Notion
- **Documentation**: Connect GitBook, Confluence, or other doc platforms  
- **File Uploads**: Upload PDFs, Word docs, or other files directly

:::warning **Important**
Your AI agent will only provide answers based on the content you provide. Make sure your website includes comprehensive information about your products, services, and policies.
:::

---

## Step 4: Test Your Agent

### Quick Test Chat

1. **Open the Test Chat**
   - Look for the "Test Chat" button in your dashboard
   - This lets you chat with your AI without customers seeing it

2. **Ask Common Questions**
   - Try questions your customers typically ask
   - Test different ways of asking the same question
   - Verify the responses are accurate and helpful

3. **Check Response Quality**
   - Are answers based on your actual content?
   - Does the tone match your brand voice?
   - Are responses complete and helpful?

### Sample Test Questions
```
- "What are your business hours?"
- "How do I return a product?"  
- "What payment methods do you accept?"
- "Can you help me track my order?"
- "I'm having trouble with [your product]"
```

## What Happens Next?

### Automatic Processing *(30 minutes)*

After you complete setup, Supportium AI will:

1. **Crawl Your Website** *(10-30 minutes)*
   - Discover all your web pages automatically
   - Extract text content and structure
   - Index information for fast searching

2. **Process Your Content** *(5-10 minutes)*
   - Analyze and organize your content
   - Create searchable knowledge base
   - Optimize for question answering

3. **Initialize Your AI Agent** *(2-5 minutes)*
   - Train the AI on your specific content
   - Configure response generation
   - Run quality and safety checks

:::info **Processing Time**
- **Small websites** (under 100 pages): 10-20 minutes
- **Medium websites** (100-500 pages): 20-45 minutes  
- **Large websites** (500+ pages): 45-90 minutes

You'll receive an email notification when processing is complete.
:::

## Troubleshooting Setup Issues

### Common Problems

**Website Can't Be Accessed**
- Ensure your website is publicly accessible
- Check that you've entered the correct URL
- Verify your site doesn't block automated crawlers

**Content Not Syncing**
- Make sure important content isn't behind login walls
- Check that your pages load properly in a regular browser
- Verify your sitemap.xml is accessible

**AI Responses Seem Inaccurate**
- Review the content that was synced from your website
- Add more detailed information to your website's help pages
- Use our custom answers feature to provide specific responses
