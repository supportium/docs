# Will it make things up?

"Hallucinations" (making things up) are a problem with AI. Supportium AI is designed to minimize this issue.

Supportium AI uses OpenAI's ChatGPT to answer questions based on your uploaded content. By default, Supportium AI only answers questions when the answer is contained in your uploaded content. It will not answer questions when the answer is not available in that content. In those cases, it may respond "I'm sorry, I don't know".

This is for 2 reasons:

  1. If it had ‘wider’ ChatGPT knowledge then there would be no way to verify the accuracy of the responses

  2. If it did have this ability then users of your Supportium AI would essentially just have a free way to access ChatGPT through your Supportium AI!

While we train our model to ensure your Supportium AI only answers questions truthfully and in a factually accurate manner, however, no service is 100% accurate and we (or anyone else) are unable to make it so and so we do not take any responsibility for the consequences of actions you take based on the results you get from your Supportium AI, you use the responses at your own risk.
